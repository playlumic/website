01What Lumic is

The depth of a video game, played with your whole body.

Health, roles, abilities, scoring, objectives and modes — moved out of the screen and into physical play. You wear the rig. The game runs on you.

The build, the tests, the things that break — and the world growing around them.

02The sport rig

The vest is the console.

You wear the rig. Light runs through it. The game reads your body — hits, holds, the ground you take — and plays out in real space.

Kinetic Combat is the platform underneath it.

Lumic is the first sport it runs. Not the last.
